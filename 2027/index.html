Sixth Asian Symposium on Cellular Automata Technology
(ASCAT 2027)

ASCAT 2027 is the sixth edition of the Asian Symposium on Cellular Automata Technology to be held during March 03-05, 2026. This time the event is being held in person. The symposium is officially organized by Institute of Mathematics and Applications, Bhubaneswar in its campus at Bhubaneswar, India. The symposium is unique by its type -- it does not look cellular automata as only mathematical tool, but also as technology. The symposium aims two-fold: to nurture the theories of cellular automata, and to explore the cellular automata as technology. So all the theoretical aspects of cellular automata and their applications in any domain are within the scope of this symposium.
